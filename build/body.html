CPA certification: the current program and its 2027 replacement

A working guide to both programs. Part A covers the current program (CPA PEP and the Common Final Examination), which ends Dec 31, 2028. Part B covers the new CPA Professional Program. Each is described the same way: structure, exams, how Ontario, Quebec, the West and Atlantic Canada run it, dates, experience and cost.

Part AThe current programCPA PEP, the Common Final Examination and practical experience. Last intake January 2027; ends Dec 31, 2028.

00The current program at a glance

The current program was developed nationally and is delivered by the same four regional bodies, with CPA Canada setting and marking the exams. Six modules lead to one three-day exam, with practical experience running alongside.[[W33,W28,BC1,W22]]

6modules: two core, two elective, two capstone[[W22]]
4module exams of four hours each[[W28,W29]]
13hCommon Final Examination, over three consecutive days[[W21]]
30moof practical experience (24 in Quebec)[[W24,QC9]]
16–24momost common time to finish the six modules[[W29,A10]]
6yrlimit for the modules and CFE in the West, Ontario and Atlantic[[W23,ON23,A10]]

What to know first

  • National exams, regional delivery. CPA Canada sets the exam dates and marks every exam; the regional bodies teach the modules and oversee experience.[[W28,W33]]
  • Everything builds to one exam. Candidates must complete all six modules before writing the three-day CFE.[[W22]]
  • The electives carry the specialization. Two of four are chosen, and Assurance plus Taxation is the public accounting route.[[W22,ON21]]
  • Experience runs separately. The 30 months can be finished before or after the CFE, within the time limit.[[W16]]
  • It closes module by module. Each module has a dated final offering, ending with the last CFE on Sep 26–28, 2028.[[W25]]

The path in one line

Prerequisites→Core 1→Core 2→Elective→Elective→Capstone 1→Capstone 2→CFE · 3 days

With 30 months of practical experience at any point along the way.[[W33,W16]]

WhenHow it ends

00The current exams and the CFE

Four module exams, one team presentation and the three-day Common Final Examination: 29 hours of written exams in all, set and marked nationally.[[W28,W21]]

The Common Final Examination, day by day

Block widths are proportional to writing time: 4, 5 and 4 hours.[[W21,W32]]

Day 1 · 4 hoursThe Capstone 1 case

Builds on the business case the candidate’s team worked through in Capstone 1.[[W32,ON22]]

Day 2 · 5 hoursDepth in one role

One case for everyone, with separate appendices for each of four roles: Assurance, Finance, Performance Management, Tax. Tests the elective area plus Financial Reporting or Management Accounting.[[ON21]]

Day 3 · 4 hoursBreadth

Tests breadth across all six technical competency areas.[[ON22,W21]]

How long each exam is

Hours of writing time.[[W28,W29,W21]]

Four module exams (16 hours) plus the CFE (13 hours) make 29 hours. The new program has 16 to 18 (our sums).

Rules that apply

  • Module exams combine objective-format questions and short cases and are written in person.[[W28,QC17]]
  • Capstone 1 is assessed by a team report and oral presentation, not an exam; Capstone 2 has no exam.[[W32,QC17]]
  • Three attempts at each exam. In Quebec a second failure means retaking the module, and a third means expulsion from the program.[[QC17]]
  • First-time CFE writers must attempt all three days in one sitting, and the CFE cannot be deferred.[[W21]]
  • The Day 2 role is declared in advance and can be any of the four, though candidates are advised to pick one matching an elective they completed.[[ON21,W32]]
  • A module exam can be deferred once the module work is complete, but the next sitting must then be attempted.[[QC17]]
  • CPA Canada publishes annual pass rates for first-time writers. CPABC reports 69.8% for the 2025 CFE.[[QC17,BC1]]
Public accounting under the current program. Complete the Tax and Assurance electives, select the Assurance role on Day 2, demonstrate depth in Assurance and Financial Reporting on the CFE, and meet the chargeable-hour experience requirements.[[ON21]]

00Experience in the current program

Thirty months of paid, relevant experience, tracked in the Practical Experience Reporting Tool (PERT), through one of two routes. It is independent of the exam sequence.[[W24,ON25,W16]]

30 months of practical experience, including up to five months away from work

Practical experience
30 months, built up in technical and enabling competencies; no Foundational / Professional split

Pre-approved program route

  • A training position approved in advance and designed to meet the requirements within 30 months.[[W24,ON25]]
  • The employer matches the candidate with a CPA mentor; less documentation is required.[[W24]]
  • Offered by public accounting firms, government and corporate employers.[[A13]]

Experience verification route

  • The candidate chooses the employer and role, and files detailed experience reports that the profession reviews.[[W24]]
  • The candidate finds their own CPA mentor.[[W24,A13]]
  • In Ontario the position is pre-assessed before reporting can begin.[[ON25]]
  • Candidates meet their CPA mentor at least twice a year.[[W24,A13]]
  • Three to 12 months of prior experience may be recognized toward the 30 months.[[W24]]
  • Quebec is different: 24 months, with at most 8 earned before registering with the Order and at least 16 after, and a report every six months.[[QC9,QC1]]
  • Current experience must be completed and reported in PERT by Dec 31, 2028; otherwise it moves to the new framework.[[ON6,W13]]

Part BThe new programThe CPA Professional Program. Rules and registration from January 2027; first modules May 2027.

01The new program at a glance

One national design, delivered four ways. The modules, exams and experience model are the same across Canada. Who teaches them, what it takes to get in, what it costs and how current students move across are set by each provincial or regional body.[[W11,N2]]

4modules, taken in order[[W5]]
4exams: one module exam and a three-part Professional Final Exam[[W6]]
16hof exams on the Common path, 18 h on the Licensure path[[N1]]
24moof experience, finished before the last module[[ON5,W9]]
3× a yearexam sittings: January, May, September[[A3,ON4]]
7yrmaximum to finish, in every region[[ON13,W11,QC1,A5]]

What to know first

  • The last exam now comes after the experience. A learner cannot register for the final module, CPA Professional Readiness, until all 24 months of experience are recognized.[[ON1,W5,QC2,A1]]
  • The three-day CFE is replaced by three Professional Final Exams, each written right after its module.[[W16,QC1]]
  • One specialization choice remains: CPA Core in a Common or a Licensure version. Licensure is required for public accounting.[[N2,ON1]]
  • Entry no longer depends on fixed prerequisite courses. Applicants show accounting knowledge through coursework or a new Knowledge Assessment.[[W16,N1]]
  • The current program runs out on Dec 31, 2028. The final CFE is September 2028.[[ON6,W14,A1]]

Four dates define the changeover

WhenWhat happens
Jan 2027Registration for the new program and the two-year transition window open.[[ON1,W1,QC1]]
May 2027First new-program modules start (West and Atlantic May 15, Ontario online May 31, Quebec Order program in May).[[W7,A2,ON2,QC1]]
Sep 15–16, 2027First new-program exams, on the same dates in every region.[[ON4,QC4,W7]]

This is why CPA Ontario says the program “launches in January 2027” and CPABC says it “launches in May 2027”. Both describe the same schedule.[[ON1,BC1,W1]]

03The new exams

The exams are national: the same papers, set to the same blueprint, on the same days in every region. The Board of Examiners sets the passing standard for each sitting, and results are determined on a national basis wherever a learner lives or writes.[[N1,A3,BC2]]

How long each exam is

Hours of writing time. Two-part exams are split where the parts are timed separately.[[N1]]

First or only partSecond part

Rules that apply to every exam

  • A module must be passed before its exam can be written.[[W5,ON2]]
  • Three attempts per module and per exam in every region, and an exam must be written within one year of passing its module.[[ON13,W11,QC1,A3]]
  • All exams are written in person, on computer, at an exam centre.[[ON12,QC1]]
  • Reference material provided: the CPA Canada Handbooks, the Income Tax Act and the Excise Tax Act. The Knowledge Assessment has none.[[N1]]
  • Testable standards and tax law reset once a year: exams from May 1 to the following April 30 use what was effective on January 1 of that year.[[N1]]
  • Only the CPA Leadership exam is disclosed after the sitting; the others are non-disclosed.[[N1]]
  • Two Specific Competencies are assessed inside the modules and not on any exam: SC4 (how standards and laws are developed and why they matter) and SC6 (the impact of technological innovation and automation).[[N1,N3]]
  • Up to 10% of objective questions on the Knowledge Assessment and the Foundational Development Exam are unscored pre-test items.[[N1]]

What each exam covers

Share of each exam by topic area, as a range. Scale 0 to 50%.

Ranges are the content allocations in the Exam Guide. “Not listed” means the blueprint gives no allocation for that area on that exam. Exam 1 · Licensure Part A uses the same cases and allocation as Exam 1 · Common. Exam 3 has no topic allocation: it tests professional judgment and ethical decision-making. Value Creation is “integrated in other topics”.[[N1]]

06Experience in the new program

The experience requirement drops to 24 months, splits into two levels, and moves ahead of the final module. One model replaces the two current routes.[[W16,ON5]]

24 months of qualifying experience, net of leaves

Foundational
up to 8 months
Professional
at least 16 months
  • Foundational experience can be entry-level but must involve decisions and judgment; it cannot be clerical. It may come from before the program starts.[[W9,W11]]
  • Professional experience counts once the learner is enrolled in Foundational Development, has a CPA mentor, and has submitted an experience plan that the provincial body approves.[[W11,ON5]]
  • All 24 months can be Professional if the role is already at that level.[[ON5,QC6]]
  • Time off, including vacation and leaves, does not count toward the 24 months.[[A1,W16]]
  • Learners must reach the top proficiency level in Financial Reporting or Management Accounting.[[W9,W11]]
  • Ontario’s task statements: three Foundational (F1–F3); Professional in Financial Reporting (FR1–FR4) or Management Accounting (MA1–MA4), plus Professional Skepticism, Ethical Decision Making and Communication (DC1–DC3). Assurance & Trust (AT1) is added for licensure.[[ON5]]
  • Training areas named by Ontario and Quebec: Financial Reporting, Management Accounting, Assurance & Trust, Taxation, Finance, Strategy & Governance, Non-Financial Reporting, Data. The Western policy lists ten, adding Value Creation and Ethical Decision-Making and Governance.[[ON5,QC6,W11]]

08Current students: moving to the new program

Placement follows one national framework: what a candidate has passed in CPA PEP decides where they enter. Eligibility, the application step and the extra time granted are regional.[[W12,A4,ON7,QC7]]

What is still required after transition

Same chart in Ontario, Quebec, the West and Atlantic
 Exempt Required Exempt unless pursuing public accounting

With two electives, Tax and Assurance together give exemption from CPA Core – Licensure; any other pair gives exemption from CPA Core – Common, and the candidate writes the matching version of Exam 1. Capstone-stage candidates are placed like two-elective candidates. All experience must still be complete before Professional Readiness.[[W12,W15,A4,ON7]]

Eligibility checklist used in the West and Atlantic

  • Program time limit ends after Dec 31, 2028.
  • Active status and good standing with the provincial body.
  • Re-enrolled for the current academic year, with no outstanding fees.
  • Experience reports in PERT up to date and reviewed.
  • Good character requirements met; no outstanding penalties or discipline.
  • In compliance with the CPA PEP training contract.

In the new program, transitioned candidates get three fresh attempts per module and exam, do not pay twice for the same requirement, and cannot return to CPA PEP.[[W12,W15,A3]]

Practical experience. In the West, candidates with less than 6 months approved by Jan 1, 2027 are steered to transition; with more than 12 months on a pre-approved route they are advised to stay. In Ontario, 8 or more months with three sub-competencies at Level 1 in PERT earns credit for the full eight months of Foundational experience.[[W15,ON8,ON14]]

10Planning notes for program managers

These points are our reading of what the official rules mean in practice. Each one rests on the cited rule; none is a statement by a CPA body.

Experience, not coursework, sets the finish date. On the Western calendar the three education modules can be finished in about a year (May 2027 to May 2028). But Professional experience only starts counting once Module 1 has begun and a plan is submitted, and at least 16 months are required. A learner who starts in May 2027 reaches 16 months in mid-September 2028, after the August 2028 Professional Readiness offering has started, so the earliest realistic Exam 3 is the January 2029 sitting.[[W7,W11,ON4]]
Put the mentor and the experience plan in place at day one. Professional experience counts no earlier than the date the plan is submitted, and none counts while a learner has no CPA mentor. Late paperwork delays the final module.[[W11]]
Mentor capacity is now a constraint. Every learner needs a CPA mentor in the same organization for Professional experience. Quebec allows a CPA from the candidate’s network when the employer has none (not for public accountancy); CPABC asks such learners to contact it.[[ON5,QC6,W11]]
Plan around three fixed exam windows. Sittings fall in mid-January, mid-May and mid-September, and module start dates are set to end at those windows. A missed window costs about four months, and an exam left unwritten for a year after its module uses up one of the three attempts.[[W7,A3,W11,ON13]]
Budget time away for in-person parts. All exams are in person, Professional Readiness is five days in person, and the West adds a two-day in-person orientation at Module 1.[[ON2,QC1,W5]]
Make the licensure decision before CPA Core. The Licensure version runs about 14 weeks against 8 for Common, has a longer exam, and requires experience in an approved firm. Switching later means a separate post-certification exam.[[ON2,N1,W11]]
Sort current candidates now. Three facts decide each case: whether the time limit runs past Dec 31, 2028, the highest PEP stage passed, and the months of experience already approved. In the West and Atlantic a candidate must apply; in Ontario the move happens on set dates whether or not the student asks, starting Jul 15, 2027 for anyone who has not passed Core 1.[[W12,W15,ON14]]
Multi-province employers need four playbooks. Admission thresholds, delivery partners, fees and transition extensions differ, and provincial rules override national ones. A learner who moves must transfer between bodies; in the West that is possible only after the Foundational Development Exam.[[W11]]
