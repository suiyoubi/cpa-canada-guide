# Reference audit, November 18, 2026

The guide's owner asked for every statement to carry a reference, with nothing guessed, and for each reference to point at the exact place in its source. This file records what was checked, what was found, and every change made.

## What was done

1. **All 140 sources were downloaded again** on Nov 18, 2026 and turned into text (PDFs page by page).
2. **Every citation was checked against its source.** The guide had 1,190 citation instances. For each one, a reviewer read the statement and the source and recorded whether the source supports it, plus a short phrase copied from the source that locates the supporting passage and, for PDFs, the page number. A script then confirmed that every phrase really is in the downloaded text and every page number is right.
3. **Every statement that was not fully supported was corrected**: reworded to what the source says, cited to the source that actually carries the fact, split by region, or removed.
4. **Text with no citation was checked too**: about 460 uncited text blocks and 255 chart labels and tooltips. Each one that states a fact now has a verified source in its own text or in the caption of its chart or table, or was reworded or removed.
5. **Citations became deep links.** A web citation opens the page at the cited passage; a PDF citation opens at the cited page. The Sources section lists every passage cited from each source.

No date, fee or exam-hour figure was found to be wrong. The problems were wording that claimed more than the source, facts cited to the wrong page, the guide's own comparisons stated as fact, and a few quotations that were not word for word.

## The corrections that matter most

- **Who approves a transition in Atlantic Canada.** The guide said CPA Atlantic approves transition applications. Its transition policy says the provincial body approves; its FAQ says the school reviews and approves. The guide now reports both and lists the inconsistency as an open item.
- **"Unfinished candidates move to the new program."** True for Ontario, whose regulation moves students on set dates. In the West and Atlantic, transition is by application and not automatic.
- **Ontario's one-year exam rule.** Regulation 9-3 says each exam must be successfully completed within one year of its module; the guide said "written".
- **Ontario's July 15, 2027 move** covers students who have the Core 1 prerequisites but have not passed Core 1, not everyone who has not passed Core 1.
- **Ontario's extra time on transition** (three years, maximum seven) is applied on January 1, 2029 and excludes two student categories.
- **Switching to the licensure path later.** The Exam Guide offers the post-certification exam only to candidates who wrote the Licensure exam and passed only Part A.
- **Quebec's final Capstone 1** ends July 21, 2028; the guide had shown the western date, July 28, for Quebec too.
- **"Same sequence by month"** for Ontario and Atlantic: both differ from the western schedule on the last Capstone dates, and the guide now says how.
- **CFE Day 3 "breadth".** No CPA source assigns breadth to Day 3 alone; the sources say Days 2 and 3 together test depth and breadth.
- **Which modules a western university may deliver.** CPAWSB has not published this, so the grid now reads "To come" for the West.
- **The guide's own judgements** ("highest academic bar", "the earliest in Quebec", "most complete rulebook", "Quebec stands apart most") were removed or replaced by the cited facts.
- **The pathway finder** no longer withholds the Core exemption from a Quebec learner with two electives passed, which the Order's transition page does not support.

## What a located passage is

For each citation the guide stores a short phrase (at most 12 words) copied from the source, and a page number for PDFs. The phrase is there to find the passage, not to reproduce it: the documents belong to their publishers. Where the evidence is a figure on a page (for example the Order's session calendar), the entry says so and points at the text beside the figure.

## Limits

- The check was made against the sources as they read on Nov 18, 2026. Pages change; the deep link then still opens the page, without the highlight.
- Statements that something is absent ("the page did not mention …") are worded as dated observations of a named page. They cannot be anchored to a passage.
- Chart labels and tooltips are plain text and cannot carry a citation of their own; their sources are in the caption or footnote of the chart, and each was verified there.
- University pages are not CPA sources. They are used only in the two sections on universities and are marked in red.
- CPA Canada's list of accredited programs carries no date of its own; "2023" is the date of the Internet Archive capture.
