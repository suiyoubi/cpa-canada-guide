CPA certification: the current program and its 2027 replacementW13

A working guide to both programs. Part A covers the current program (CPA PEP and the Common Final Examination), which ends Dec 31, 2028.W13 Part B covers the new CPA Professional Program. Each is described the same way: structure, exams, how Ontario, Quebec, the West and Atlantic Canada run it, dates, experience and cost.

01The current program at a glance

The current program was developed nationally and is delivered by provincial or regional bodies, with CPA Canada setting the exam dates and marking all exams. Six modules lead to one three-day exam, with practical experience running alongside.W33W28BC1W22

What to know first

  • National exams, regional delivery. CPA Canada sets the exam dates and marks every exam; provincial or regional bodies deliver the program. In the West, CPAWSB delivers CPA PEP and the provincial bodies administer the experience requirements.W28BC1W33
  • Everything builds to one exam. Candidates must complete all six modules before writing the three-day CFE.W22
  • The electives carry the specialization. Two of four are chosen, and Assurance plus Taxation is the public accounting route.W22ON21
  • Experience runs separately. The 30 months can be finished before or after the CFE, within the time limit.W16
  • It closes module by module. Each module has a dated final offering, ending with the last CFE on Sep 26–28, 2028.W25

03The current exams and the CFE

Four module exams, one team presentation and the three-day Common Final Examination: 29 hours of written exams in all (our sum: four 4-hour module exams and 13 CFE hours).W28W21 CPA Canada sets the exam dates and marks all exams.W28

The Common Final Examination, day by day

Block widths are proportional to writing time: 4, 5 and 4 hours.W21W32

Day 1 · 4 hoursThe Capstone 1 case

Builds on the business case the candidate’s team worked through in Capstone 1.W32ON22

Day 2 · 5 hoursDepth in one role

One case for everyone, with separate appendices for each of four roles: Assurance, Finance, Performance Management, Tax. Tests the elective area plus Financial Reporting or Management Accounting.ON21

Day 3 · 4 hoursWith Day 2: depth and breadth

CPAWSB says Days 2 and 3 test the depth and breadth of candidates’ competency development. CPA Ontario says the three-day CFE requires breadth across all six technical competencies.W21ON22

How long each exam is

Hours of writing time.W28W29W21 Candidates choose two of the four electives; Day 2 tests depth in the declared elective role, and Days 2 and 3 together test depth and breadth.W22W32W21

Four module exams (16 hours) plus the CFE (13 hours) make 29 hours.W29W32 The new program has 16 or 18, depending on the version of the CPA Core exam: 5 hours for Foundational Development, 3 or 5 for CPA Core, 5 for CPA Leadership and 3 for Professional Readiness.N1 Both totals are our sums.

Rules that apply

  • Module exams combine objective-format questions and short cases and are written in person.W28QC17
  • Capstone 1 is assessed by a team report and oral presentation, not an exam; Capstone 2 has no exam.W32QC17
  • Three attempts at each exam. In Quebec a second failure means retaking the module, and a third means expulsion from the program.QC17
  • First-time CFE writers must attempt all three days in one sitting, and the CFE cannot be deferred.W21
  • The Day 2 role is declared in advance and can be any of the four, though candidates are advised to pick one matching an elective they completed.ON21W32
  • A module exam can be deferred once the module work is complete, but the next sitting must then be attempted.QC17
  • CPA Canada publishes annual pass rates for first-time writers. CPABC reports 69.8% for the 2025 CFE.QC17BC1
Public accounting under the current program. Complete the Tax and Assurance electives, select the Assurance role on Day 2, demonstrate depth in Assurance and Financial Reporting on the CFE, and meet the chargeable-hour experience requirements.ON21

07Experience in the current program

Thirty months of paid, relevant experience, tracked in the Practical Experience Reporting Tool (PERT), through one of two routes. It is independent of the exam sequence.W24ON25W16

30 months of practical experience, including up to five months away from workW24W16

Practical experience
30 months, developing breadth and depth of technical competencies and enabling competenciesW24W24

Pre-approved program route

  • A training position approved in advance and designed to meet the requirements within 30 months.W24ON25
  • The employer matches the candidate with a CPA mentor; less documentation is required.W24
  • Offered by public accounting firms, government and corporate employers.A13

Experience verification route

  • The candidate chooses the employer and role, and files detailed experience reports that the profession reviews.W24
  • The candidate finds their own CPA mentor.W24A13
  • In Ontario the position is pre-assessed before reporting can begin.ON25
  • Candidates meet their CPA mentor at least twice a year.W24A13
  • Three to 12 months of prior experience may be recognized toward the 30 months.W24

  • CPA Ontario gives December 2028 as the final date to complete experience requirements in PERT. Eligible candidates can transition their experience to the new program; in the West this is by application.ON6W13

09The new program at a glance

The new program consists of the Foundational Development module and three professional modules, with a Knowledge Assessment at entry for learners who are not exempt. The CPA profession strives for national consistency, but provincial regulations and bylaws take precedence over national requirements, so learners must refer to the regulations of their own provincial body.W11N2

What to know first

  • The last exam comes after the experience. A learner cannot start the final module, CPA Professional Readiness, until all 24 months of experience are complete.ON1W5QC2A1
  • The three-day CFE is replaced by three Professional Final Exams, each written right after its module.W16QC1
  • CPA Core has two pathways: Common or Licensure. Those seeking a public accounting licence must pass the Licensure version.N2ON1
  • Entry no longer depends on fixed prerequisite courses. Applicants show accounting knowledge through coursework or a new Knowledge Assessment.W16N1
  • The current program runs out on Dec 31, 2028. The final CFE is September 2028.ON6W14A1

11The new exams

The exams are national and will be consistent across the country, each with its own blueprint. The Board of Examiners sets the passing standard for each exam, and results are determined on a national basis regardless of where learners live or the language in which they write.N1A3BC2

How long each exam is

Hours of writing time. Exams with two parts are shown split. The time for each part is controlled in the Knowledge Assessment and the CPA Core – Licensure exam; in the Professional Readiness exam, candidates manage the time between the two cases themselves.N1

First or only partSecond part

Rules that apply to every exam

  • A module must be passed before its exam can be written.W5ON2
  • Three attempts per module and per exam in Ontario, the West and Atlantic; Quebec states three attempts at each part of the Professional Final Exam. An exam must be attempted within one year of completing its module in the West and Quebec, and successfully completed within one year in Ontario; if not, an attempt is counted.ON13W11QC1A3
  • All exams are written in person, on computer, at an exam centre.ON12QC1
  • Reference material provided: the CPA Canada Handbooks, the Income Tax Act and the Excise Tax Act. The Knowledge Assessment has none.N1
  • Testable standards and tax law reset once a year: exams from May 1 to the following April 30 use what was effective on January 1 of that year.N1
  • Only the CPA Leadership exam is disclosed after the sitting; the others are non-disclosed.N1
  • Two Specific Competencies are assessed inside the modules and not on any exam: SC4 (how standards and laws are developed and why they matter) and SC6 (the impact of technological innovation and automation).N1N3
  • Up to 10% of objective questions on the Knowledge Assessment and the Foundational Development Exam are unscored pre-test items.N1

What each exam covers

Share of each exam by topic area, as a range. Scale 0 to 50%.

Ranges are the content allocations in the Exam Guide. “Not listed” means the blueprint gives no allocation for that area on that exam. Exam 1 · Licensure Part A uses the same cases and allocation as Exam 1 · Common. Exam 3 has no topic allocation: it tests professional judgment and ethical decision-making. Value Creation is “integrated in other topics”.N1

19Planning notes for program managers

These points are our reading of what the official rules mean in practice. Each one rests on the cited rule; none is a statement by a CPA body.

Plan the finish date around experience, not coursework. On CPAWSB’s schedule the first Foundational Development offering starts on May 15, 2027 and a CPA Leadership exam falls on May 17, 2028; our reading is that the three education modules can be finished in about a year.W7W7 In the West, Professional experience can be accumulated only once the learner is enrolled in Foundational Development, has a mentor and has submitted an experience plan, and at least 16 months are required.W11W11 By our arithmetic, a learner who starts in May 2027 reaches 16 months in mid-September 2028 at the earliest, after CPAWSB’s Professional Readiness offering of Aug 19, 2028 has started; experience must be met before that module.W7W16 As of Nov 18, 2026 CPAWSB’s schedule showed no dates in 2029.W7 CPA Ontario’s schedule lists an Exam 3 sitting on Jan 18, 2029; that date is Ontario’s, not CPAWSB’s.ON4
Put the mentor and the experience plan in place at day one. In the West, Professional experience counts no earlier than the date the plan is submitted, and none is recognized while a learner has no CPA mentor.W11W11 Our reading: late paperwork delays the final module, because experience must be met before Professional Readiness.W16
Check mentor capacity early. In Ontario and the West, the CPA mentor must work in the same organization as the learner.ON5W11 Quebec allows a CPA from the candidate’s network when the employer has none (not for public accountancy); British Columbia learners with no CPA mentor at their workplace are told to contact CPABC.QC6W11
Plan around the three exam windows each year. Exams are offered in January, May and September, and each module offering on CPAWSB’s schedule ends with one of those exam dates.A3W7 A missed window costs about four months (our arithmetic from those months). In the West an exam not attempted within one year of its module uses up one of the three attempts; in Ontario an attempt is counted if the exam is not successfully completed within that year.W11ON13
Budget time away for in-person parts. All exams are in person, Professional Readiness is five days in person, and the West adds a two-day in-person orientation at Module 1.ON2QC1W5
Make the licensure decision before CPA Core. In Ontario the Licensure version runs 14 weeks against 8 for Common. Its exam is five hours against three, and in the West licensure requires 24 months of experience in a registered, licensed CPA firm. A candidate who writes the Licensure exam and passes only Part A can continue in the program and will have a later chance, after certification, to write a separate exam for licensure.ON2N1W11
Sort current candidates now. Check three things for each case: whether the time limit runs past Dec 31, 2028, what has been completed in CPA PEP, and the experience recognized to date.W12W12 In the West and Atlantic a candidate must apply; in Ontario, CPA Ontario moves students on set dates, starting Jul 15, 2027 for those who have completed the Core 1 prerequisites but have not passed Core 1.W15A4ON14
Multi-province employers must check each provincial body’s rules. Provincial regulations and bylaws take precedence over national requirements, and organizations operating in several jurisdictions must refer to the regulations of each one. A learner must stay registered with the body where they live.W11 In the West, a transfer to another body is possible only after the Foundational Development Exam is passed.W11

20Side by side: how the new program differs from the current one

The comparison below uses CPAWSB’s official program comparison and the Order’s comparison chart. The current program is CPA PEP, the Common Final Examination and the practical experience requirement.W16QC9W12

DimensionCurrent program (to Dec 31, 2028)New CPA Professional Program (from 2027)
Current path

Prerequisites → Core 1 → Core 2 → Elective → Elective → Capstone 1 → Capstone 2 → three-day CFE; 30 months of experience at any point within the time limit.W16BC1

New path

Knowledge shown → Foundational Development + exam → CPA Core + Exam 1 → CPA Leadership + Exam 2 → 24 months of experience complete → Professional Readiness + Exam 3.W16N2
